# ZaibWall

**Beautiful wallpapers. Zero clutter.**

ZaibWall is a lightweight, wallpaper-first Flutter application for Android and iOS. Its catalog is delivered online, while Saved, Recently Used, and cached content remain useful offline.

## Product direction

- Modern premium UI with restrained glassmorphism
- No generic purple/AI-dashboard aesthetic
- AI-generated wallpapers as content, not as the visual identity
- No account required for v1
- Small application package; wallpapers are not bundled into the app
- Thumbnail/preview/full-resolution image pipeline
- Offline-first local experience
- Android + iOS
- AdMob monetization with controlled frequency
- Free/open-source technology and free service tiers while pre-revenue, subject to provider limits
